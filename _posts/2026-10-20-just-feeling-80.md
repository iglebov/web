<pre class="poem">Just feeling 🐧
When did dreams fade away
Of being someone I am not today?
Just playing ball
And not worrying at all.
Life’s sobering drink
Which urges one to think.
There is no secret meaning,
So that’s left is just feeling.
Earning different emotions
Of all kind of portions.
Aligning inner compas
While staying conscious.
Endless amount of paths
Are opened to us.
Everyone is his own wright
And deciding what is right.</pre>
